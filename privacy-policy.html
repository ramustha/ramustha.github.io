Privacy Policy for Zayn Storybook

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Welcome to Zayn Storybook ("we", "our", or "us"). We create animated educational children's storybooks and publish video content across digital platforms including TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.

1. Information Collection and Use

Zayn Storybook is strictly committed to data privacy and user safety:

  • No Personal Data Collection: Our application does not collect, record, harvest, sell, or share personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • Platform Analytics: We only access aggregated, anonymized metrics provided directly by platform APIs (e.g., aggregate view counts, likes, and comments) to track content performance.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our content is designed for families and young children:

  • We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 13.

3. Third-Party Platform APIs

Our publishing tools interact with trusted platform APIs governed by their respective privacy terms:

  • TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/privacy-policy/en
  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram): https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Google / YouTube: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

Zayn Storybook does not maintain external user databases or store your personal data on our servers. If you wish to remove any interactions (likes or comments) made on our social media profiles, you may delete them directly through your platform account at any time.
